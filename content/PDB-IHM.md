[[wwPDB]] Integrative and Hybrid Methods Task Force

Managed by the [[wwPDB]] and funded by the NSF, DOE, and NIH.

In 2014, the [wwPDB](http://www.wwpdb.org/) convened an [Integrative/Hybrid Methods (IHM) Task Force](http://www.wwpdb.org/task/hybrid) and sponsored a Workshop held at the European Bioinformatics Institute (EBI). The purpose of the Workshop was to engage a community of experts to make recommendations for how to responsibly archive integrative structures.

A white paper was published ([Sali et al., 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.str.2015.05.013)) and two working groups were established: the Federation Working Group was to address the issues of data federation and the Model Working Group was tasked with helping set up the framework for model representation, validation, and visualization.

The PDB-IHM is a wwPDB project developed by a multi-disciplinary team of researchers and developers from RCSB PDB at Rutgers, the State University of New Jersey, University of California San Francisco, and University of Southern California.
