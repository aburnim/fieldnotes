> [!info] Info
> Diamond Light Source is the UK's national synchrotron facility, located at the Harwell Science and Innovation Campus, Oxfordshire. Produces intense beams of X-rays, UV, and infrared light. Seven MX beamlines plus the electron Bio-Imaging Centre (eBIC) for cryo-EM. ~10,000 researcher visits per year. Co-located with [[CCP4]] core team at the Research Complex at Harwell.

## Key Relationships
- Home of [[DIALS]] development (UK side); [[Gwyndaf Evans]] leads MX group
- [[CCP4]] core team ([[Eugene Krissinel]], [[Charles Ballard]]) based at Research Complex at Harwell, same campus
- eBIC provides cryo-EM access through [[Instruct-ERIC]]
- XChem fragment screening facility on I04-1 beamline
- [[Robert Rambo]] ([[ScAtter]] developer) is at Diamond
- [[Peter Chang]] ([[NIAC]] Documentation Release Manager) is at Diamond
- ISPyB information management system used across European synchrotrons
