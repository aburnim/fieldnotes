> [!info] Info
> EPU is Thermo Fisher Scientific's proprietary automated data acquisition software for their Krios, Glacios, and Tundra cryo-TEM platforms. Commercial, closed-source. Integrates with Embedded [[cryoSPARC]] Live for real-time processing.

> [!warning] Commercial lock-in
> EPU is bundled with Thermo Fisher microscopes, creating a vertically integrated workflow: EPU (acquisition) + Embedded CryoSPARC Live (processing). This is a significant commercial dynamics point. [[SerialEM]] is the open-source alternative.

## Key Relationships
- [[Thermo Fisher Scientific]] proprietary
- Embedded [[cryoSPARC]] Live integration
- Competes with [[SerialEM]] for acquisition control
