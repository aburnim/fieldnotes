> [!info] Info
> NSLS-II is a synchrotron facility at Brookhaven National Laboratory (Upton, NY). DOE Office of Science user facility. Successor to the original NSLS. Features multiple structural biology beamlines including AMX and FMX for macromolecular crystallography, and LiX for SAXS/WAXS.

## Key Relationships
- [[Thomas Caswell]] (Brookhaven/NSLS-II) is a [[NIAC]] member
- LiX beamline relevant for SAXS/WAXS (connects to [[SASBDB]], [[ATSAS suite]])
- AMX/FMX beamlines for MX
- Data processed with [[DIALS]], [[XDS]]
- [[NIAC]] 2018 meeting hosted at NSLS-II

## Funding
- DOE Office of Science (primary)
- NIH (structural biology beamlines)
