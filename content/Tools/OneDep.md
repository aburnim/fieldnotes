#wwpdb #mx #cryo-em #nmr #validation

> [!info] Info
> OneDep is the [[wwPDB]] unified system for structure deposition, validation, and biocuration. All structures deposited to the PDB, EMDB, and BMRB pass through OneDep. The system runs validation checks (using tools from [[MolProbity]], [[PHENIX]], [[CCP4]], and others) and generates the wwPDB validation reports. Per the 2025 wwPDB AC report, retiring OneDep's technical debt is the top strategic priority for the [[wwPDB]].
