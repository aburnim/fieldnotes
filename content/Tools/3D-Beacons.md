> [!info] Info
> 3D-Beacons is a federated network providing unified access to both experimental and computationally predicted protein structure models. Hub hosted at [[EMBL-EBI]], led by [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]]. Aggregates data from: PDBe, AlphaFold DB, [[SWISS-MODEL]], Protein Ensemble Database (PED), [[SASBDB]], Genome3D, and PDBe-KB. Users query via UniProt accession numbers.

- AlphaFold DB (also at EMBL-EBI) is a major provider

> [!faq] Why this matters
> 3D-Beacons is the first federated attempt to unify experimental and predicted structural models under one API. It represents the emerging governance model for how predicted structures (AlphaFold, SWISS-MODEL) interact with experimental data (PDB, EMDB, SASBDB).
