> [!info] Info
> BioXTAS RAW is an open-source program for biological SAXS data reduction and analysis. Developed by [[Jesse Hopkins]] at BioCAT (Bio-CATS sector 18, Advanced Photon Source, Argonne). GUI-based, Python. Complements/alternative to [[ATSAS suite]].
