#mx #software-dev

> [!info] Info
> Data reduction and scaling suite for X-ray diffraction data. Consists of DENZO (integration), SCALEPACK (merging and scaling), and the HKL-2000 / HKL-3000 GUI. Dominated MX data processing at synchrotron beamlines worldwide through the 2000s and 2010s.

- [[Wladek Minor]] ([[University of Virginia]]) -- co-developer
