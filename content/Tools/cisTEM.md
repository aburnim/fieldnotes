> [!info] Info
> cisTEM (computational imaging system for transmission electron microscopy) is a user-friendly cryo-EM processing suite. Developed by [[Timothy Grant]] and [[Nikolaus Grigorieff]]. Emphasis on ease of use and GUI-driven workflow.
