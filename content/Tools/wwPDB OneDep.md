> [!info] Info
> OneDep is the unified deposition, validation, and biocuration system for the [[wwPDB]]. All depositions to PDB, [[EMDB]], and [[BMRB]] go through OneDep. Hosted at wwPDB data centers worldwide (US, Europe, Asia). Developed collaboratively by [[RCSB PDB]], [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]], [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]], and the [[EMDB]] team.

## Key Relationships
- Central infrastructure for all [[wwPDB]] depositions
- Biocuration split geographically: RCSB (Americas/Oceania), PDBe/EMDB (Europe/Africa), PDBj/PDBc (Asia)
- Validation tools include [[MolProbity]] criteria, EM validation metrics
- Integration with [[SASBDB]] OneDep line underway
- Uses PDBx/mmCIF format ([[History of mmcif working group]])
