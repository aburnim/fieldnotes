> [!info] Info
> SerialEM is the dominant software for automated data acquisition on transmission electron microscopes. Developed by [[David Mastronarde]] at the University of Colorado Boulder. Used for both single-particle cryo-EM and electron tomography. Free for academic use.

## People
| Name | Role | Affiliation |
|------|------|-------------|
| [[David Mastronarde]] | Developer / maintainer | University of Colorado Boulder |

## Key Relationships
- Controls data collection on most cryo-EM microscopes worldwide
- Feeds data into [[RELION]], [[cryoSPARC]], [[EMAN2]] processing pipelines
- Competes with [[EPU]] (Thermo Fisher proprietary) for automated acquisition
