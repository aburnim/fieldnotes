> ARIA (Ambiguous Restraints for Iterative Assignment) and CYANA (Combined assignment and dYnamics Algorithm for NMR Applications) are the two dominant NMR structure calculation programs. They automate NOE assignment and structure determination.

### ARIA
- Developed by [[Michael Nilges]] and [[Benjamin Bardiaux]] at Institut Pasteur, Paris
- Uses CNS for structure calculation
- Open source

### CYANA
- Developed by [[Peter Guntert]], originally at ETH Zurich, now at Goethe University Frankfurt
- Proprietary license (free for academic)
- Torsion angle dynamics approach

## Key Relationships
- Both produce structures deposited in [[RCSB PDB]] / [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]]
- Restraint data deposited in [[BMRB]]
- [[Peter Guntert]] is at Goethe University Frankfurt, same institution as [[Harald Schwalbe]] ([[Instruct-ERIC]] Director)
