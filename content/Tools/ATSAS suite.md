> [!info] Info
> ATSAS is the dominant software suite for small-angle X-ray and neutron scattering (SAXS/SANS) data analysis. Developed by [[Dmitri Svergun]]'s Biological Small Angle Scattering group at [[EMBL]] Hamburg. Includes DAMMIF (ab initio shape reconstruction), CRYSOL (scattering calculation from atomic models), PRIMUS (data processing), and many others. 6,000+ registered users. Free for academic use.
