> [!info] Info
> NMRPipe is a multidimensional NMR spectral processing system based on Unix pipes. Created by Frank Delaglio at NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology). Includes NMRDraw for visualization. One of the most widely used NMR processing tools. Free for academic use.
