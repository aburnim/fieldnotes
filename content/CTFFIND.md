> [!info] Info
> CTFFIND is the standard tool for estimating the contrast transfer function (CTF) from cryo-EM micrographs. Developed by [[Nikolaus Grigorieff]] and [[Alexis Rohou]]. Used as a preprocessing step in virtually all cryo-EM pipelines including [[RELION]], [[cryoSPARC]], [[Scipion]].
