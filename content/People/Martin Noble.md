#mx #computational #software-dev

Newcastle University / [[CCP4]]

> [!info] Info
> Professor of Structural Biology and Anti-Cancer Drug Design at Newcastle University, and Head of Academic Structural Sciences with Cancer Research Horizons - Therapeutic Innovations. Research is on structure-based discovery of cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) inhibitors. Member of the [[CCP4]] Executive Committee and lead of **CCP4i2**, the graphical user interface to the CCP4 program suite.
