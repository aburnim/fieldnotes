[[CCP4]] / [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]] / CCP-EM

> [!info] Info
> Leads the Computational Biology theme at STFC Scientific Computing (~35 staff), based at the [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]], Harwell. Background in theoretical chemistry and condensed matter physics; joined [[CCP4]] in 1995 as a programmer at Daresbury Laboratory, became [[CCP4]] Group Leader in 2007. Contributed TLS refinement in REFMAC and the automated molecular replacement pipelines [[MrBUMP]] and AMPLE. Co-created CCP-EM in 2012 and is PI on supporting MRC grants for that consortium.
