Novartis

> [!info] Info
> Structural biologist at Novartis (Novartis Institutes for BioMedical Research). Serves as the industrial structural biology representative on the [[wwPDB]] Scientific Advisory Committee, representing the pharma user community of the [[RCSB PDB]].

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Advisory Committee member, [[RCSB PDB]] representative. Provides the pharmaceutical industry perspective on PDB use, deposition, and validation requirements. Industrial representation on the AC is important because pharma is among the largest user communities of the PDB for structure-based drug discovery.
