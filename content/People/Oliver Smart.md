#mx #computational #software-dev

[[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)]] (was [[Global Phasing Ltd]])

> [!info] Info
> Crystallographer at [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]], [[EMBL-EBI]], at the time of the 2015 Ligand Validation Workshop. Previously at [[Global Phasing Ltd]] where he contributed to [[BUSTER-TNT]] refinement and developed the Conformation-Independent Structural Alignment tools. Also the original author of HOLE, the pore-profile analysis software for ion channels.
