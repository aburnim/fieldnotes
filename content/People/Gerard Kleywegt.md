[[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)]] / [[EMBL-EBI]] (was Uppsala University at time of XVTF)

> [!info] Info
> Team leader at [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]], [[EMBL-EBI]]. Moved from [[Uppsala University]] in 2009 to take on wwPDB / PDBe leadership in Hinxton. Formerly developer of the Uppsala Software Factory (MOLEMAN, MAPMAN, LSQMAN, ODB tools) and a long-standing voice for structural data quality.

## Key Relationships
- [[Sameer Velankar]] and [[Anastassis Perrakis]] -- PDBe / wwPDB peers
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member until May 2009 (Read et al., Structure 2011)
- Uppsala Software Factory (MOLEMAN, MAPMAN, LSQMAN, ODB) -- historical tool suite developed during his Uppsala years, still used across the MX community
