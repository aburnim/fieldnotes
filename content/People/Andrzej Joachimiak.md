[[APS]] / Argonne National Laboratory

> [!info] Info
> Distinguished Fellow and Director of the Structural Biology Center (SBC) at the [[APS|Advanced Photon Source]], Argonne National Laboratory. The SBC operates the 19-ID and 19-BM macromolecular crystallography beamlines and was historically the technical core of the Midwest Center for Structural Genomics (MCSG), one of the NIH Protein Structure Initiative centers.
