#mx #computational #software-dev

[[University of Cambridge]] / CIMR

> [!info] Info
> Professor of Protein Crystallography at the Cambridge Institute for Medical Research (CIMR), [[University of Cambridge]]. Lead developer of [[Phaser]], the maximum-likelihood molecular replacement program bundled with [[PHENIX]] and [[CCP4]]. President of the [[IUCr]] (elected 2023).

## Key Relationships
- [[Phaser]] -- principal developer; maximum-likelihood molecular replacement
- [[PHENIX]] consortium member alongside [[Paul Adams]], [[Tom Terwilliger]], [[Jane S. Richardson]]
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member (Read et al., Structure 2011)
- [[IUCr]] -- President
