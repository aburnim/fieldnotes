#mx #computational #software-dev

[[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] / [[PHENIX|PHENIX Consortium]]

> [!info] Info
> Scientist in the MBIB Division at [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]] and core [[PHENIX]] developer. Principal author of **eLBOW** (Electronic Ligand Builder and Optimisation Workbench), the ligand geometry and restraint generation tool used across PHENIX for handling non-protein components including drug candidates. Also central to PHENIX restraint libraries and carbohydrate handling.
