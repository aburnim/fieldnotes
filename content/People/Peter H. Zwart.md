#mx #sas #computational #software-dev #beamline

[[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] / [[ALS]]

> [!info] Info
> Staff scientist at the Molecular Biophysics and Integrated Bioimaging (MBIB) Division of [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] and Berkeley Center for Structural Biology beamline scientist at the [[ALS|Advanced Light Source]]. Core author of the Computational Crystallography Toolbox (cctbx) and [[PHENIX]]. Also developed tools for small-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS) data analysis on ALS SAXS beamlines (e.g., SIBYLS 12.3.1).
