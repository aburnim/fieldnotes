OpenEye Scientific

> [!info] Info
> Founder, President, and CEO of OpenEye Scientific (founded 1997; acquired by Cadence Design Systems in 2022 and now operating as OpenEye, Cadence Molecular Sciences). OpenEye produces molecular modeling and cheminformatics software for drug discovery: ROCS (shape-based overlays), FRED and HYBRID (docking), Omega (conformers), QUACPAC (tautomers/protomers), SZMAP and ZAP (electrostatics). Early and vocal advocate for quality in computational chemistry.
