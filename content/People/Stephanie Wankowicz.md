> [!info] Info
> Scientific Program Director for DiffUSE (listed on diffuse.science/members) and Assistant Professor of Molecular Physiology and Biophysics at Vanderbilt University. Previously a postdoc in the [[Jaime Fraser]] lab at [[UCSF]], where she worked on multi-conformer modeling and contributed to **qFit** development. Her research combines computational and experimental approaches to understand protein conformational heterogeneity from X-ray crystallography and cryo-EM data.

> [!faq] DiffUSE role
> Stephanie Wankowicz is the DiffUSE Scientific Program Director at Radial (listed on diffuse.science/members). She bridges the Fraser Lab / qFit lineage with the operational leadership of DiffUSE. Her expertise in multi-conformer modeling from X-ray data positions her at the center of the diffuse scattering data-to-model pipeline.
