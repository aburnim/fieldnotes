[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] / [[MRC-LMB]]

> [!info] Info
> Group leader in the Structural Studies Division at the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology|MRC-LMB]], Cambridge. Principal author of **REFMAC5**, the maximum-likelihood macromolecular refinement program in [[CCP4]] used across MX and (more recently) cryo-EM map refinement. REFMAC has been one of the two dominant refinement packages worldwide (alongside [[PHENIX|phenix.refine]]).
