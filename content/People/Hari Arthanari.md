#nmr #wwpdb

[[Dana-Farber Cancer Institute]] / Harvard Medical School

> [!info] Info
> Associate Professor in the Department of Biological Chemistry and Molecular Pharmacology (BCMP) at Harvard Medical School, with a primary appointment in the Department of Cancer Biology at the [[Dana-Farber Cancer Institute]]. Research focus: mapping hotspots at protein-protein interaction (PPI) interfaces using solution NMR spectroscopy, NMR-based fragment and high-throughput screening, and biophysical and cell-based assays. Serves as Co-Chair of the [[wwPDB]] Scientific Advisory Committee alongside [[John Rubinstein]].

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Co-Chair of the [[wwPDB]] Scientific Advisory Committee; [[BMRB]] representative on the AC.
