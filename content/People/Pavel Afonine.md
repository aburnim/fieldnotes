#mx #cryo-em #computational #software-dev

[[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] / [[PHENIX|PHENIX Consortium]]

> [!info] Info
> Scientist in the Molecular Biophysics and Integrated Bioimaging (MBIB) Division at [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]] and a core developer of [[PHENIX]]. Principal architect of **phenix.refine**, the refinement engine used for X-ray, neutron, and cryo-EM structure refinement across the PHENIX suite.

## Key Relationships
- [[Paul Adams]] -- current PHENIX consortium lead at LBNL
- **phenix.refine** -- principal author
- [[PHENIX|PHENIX Consortium]] -- LBNL core team alongside [[Nigel Moriarty]], [[Dorothee Liebschner]], [[Billy Poon]], [[Oleg Sobolev]], [[Peter H. Zwart]]
