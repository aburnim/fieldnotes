[[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] / [[PHENIX|PHENIX Consortium]]

> [!info] Info
> Scientist in the MBIB Division at [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]] and core developer of the Computational Crystallography Toolbox (**cctbx**) - the open-source foundation underneath [[PHENIX]]. Responsible for significant portions of the PHENIX build infrastructure, release tooling, and low-level crystallographic mathematics.
