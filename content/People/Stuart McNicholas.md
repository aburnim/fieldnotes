#mx #cryo-em #computational #software-dev

[[University of York]] / [[CCP4]]

> [!info] Info
> [[CCP4]] funded developer at the [[University of York]], funded under the STFC/CCP4 Collaboration Agreement for development of CCP4mg and the CCP4i2 project. Part of the **Moorhen** team that compiled [[Coot]] and CCP4 libraries to WebAssembly to produce a web browser molecular graphics program with a React user interface.

## Key Relationships
- [[CCP4]] funded developer (CCP4mg / CCP4i2 lineage)
- **Moorhen** team: also [[Martin Noble]], Filomeno Sanchez Rodriguez, Lucrezia Catapano
- [[Paul Emsley]] -- [[Coot]] desktop program that Moorhen is built on
- [[University of York]] / [[Jon Agirre]] (YSBL colleagues)
