#mx #cryo-em #computational #software-dev

University of Hamburg / Excellence Cluster CUI

> [!info] Info
> Group leader in the Excellence Cluster CUI (Advanced Imaging of Matter) at the Institute of Nanostructures and Solid State Physics, University of Hamburg. Developer of **AUSPEX** (diffraction diagnostics tool for X-ray data) and **HARUSPEX** (neural network for automatic secondary structure annotation of cryo-EM maps). Led the global **Coronavirus Structural Task Force** during the COVID-19 pandemic, which coordinated re-processing and validation of SARS-CoV-2-related structures.

> [!faq] Handoff context
> Thorn is one of the most visible current-generation CCP4-adjacent figures: she trained under George Sheldrick (SHELX lineage) at Göttingen, contributed to SHELX / ANODE / PHASER historically, and now runs an independent group applying ML to validation. The Coronavirus Structural Task Force established her as a community organizer.
