[[CCP4]] / [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]]

> [!info] Info
> Project leader of [[CCP4]] (Collaborative Computational Project Number 4), based at the [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]], Harwell. Developer of core CCP4 infrastructure including GESAMT, PDBeFold (SSM), and the CCP4 Cloud platform. Funded historically by [[BBSRC]] and [[EMBL]].
