[[Rutgers]]

> [!info] Info
> University Professor and Henry Rutgers Chair at [[Rutgers]], The State University of New Jersey, where he serves as Director of the [[RCSB PDB]], the Institute for Quantitative Biomedicine, and the Rutgers Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (RAD) Collaboratory. Also a member of the Rutgers Cancer Institute of New Jersey, where he co-leads the Cancer Pharmacology Research Program. Expert in structural biology, proteomics, bioinformatics, structure- and fragment-based drug discovery, and clinical oncology, with more than 370 publications and over 1,200 experimental structures deposited in the PDB.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Principal Investigator for [[RCSB PDB]], the US member of the [[wwPDB]] consortium. In this role he is the senior scientific lead for PDB operations in the Americas and has been central to wwPDB governance through multiple AC cycles.
