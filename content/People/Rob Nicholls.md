#mx #cryo-em #computational #software-dev

[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] / [[CCP4]]

> [!info] Info
> Scientist in the [[Garib Murshudov]] group at the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology|MRC-LMB]], Cambridge. Lead developer of **ProSMART** (PROcrustes Structural Matching Alignment and Restraints Tool) for conformation-independent structure comparison and generation of interatomic distance restraints for REFMAC5 refinement. Core contributor to recent REFMAC5 developments including neutron crystallographic refinement. [[CCP4]] developer/contributor.

> [!faq] Handoff context
> Nicholls is the current-generation continuity of the REFMAC development lineage, working directly with [[Garib Murshudov]] at MRC-LMB and bringing REFMAC into new regimes (neutron crystallography, cryo-EM).
