> Sir Tom Blundell, FRS FMedSci. Emeritus Professor of Biochemistry at [[University of Cambridge]]. Director of the [[Erice International School of Crystallography]]. Former Chair of [[BBSRC]] (2009-2015). Former head of the Department of Biochemistry at Cambridge. One of the foundational figures in protein crystallography and structure-based drug design.
