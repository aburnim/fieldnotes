#mx #computational #software-dev #fragment-screening

Astex Pharmaceuticals (was Astex Therapeutics at time of XVTF)

> [!info] Info
> Senior crystallographer at **Astex Pharmaceuticals** (formerly Astex Therapeutics), Cambridge UK / Pleasanton USA. Contributor to the **BUSTER-TNT** maximum-likelihood refinement package (with [[Gerard Bricogne]] at [[Global Phasing Ltd]]). A central figure in applying high-throughput X-ray crystallography to fragment-based drug discovery at Astex, a pioneer of the method.

## Key Relationships
- [[Gerard Bricogne]] / [[Global Phasing Ltd]] -- BUSTER-TNT collaborators
- Fragment-based drug discovery community intersects with XChem at [[Diamond Light Source]] and [[Frank von Delft]] ([[Structural Genomics Consortium]])
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member (Read et al., Structure 2011)
