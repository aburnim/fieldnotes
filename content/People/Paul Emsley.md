#mx #cryo-em #computational #software-dev

[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] (was [[University of Oxford]] at time of XVTF)

> [!info] Info
> Structural biologist and principal author of [[Coot]], the interactive macromolecular-model building program used ubiquitously in MX and cryo-EM. At the time of the 2011 X-Ray Validation Task Force he was at the Department of Biochemistry, [[University of Oxford]]; since moved to the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] in Cambridge.

## Key Relationships
- [[Coot]] -- principal author; integrates validation from [[MolProbity]] and [[PDB-REDO]]
- [[Kevin Cowtan]] -- collaborator on density/model fitting tools
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member (Read et al., Structure 2011)
- [[CCP4]] community
