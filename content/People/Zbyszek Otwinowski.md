#leader #mx #computational #software-dev

UT Southwestern Medical Center

> [!info] Info
> Professor of Biophysics at UT Southwestern Medical Center, Dallas. Co-developer of the **HKL / DENZO / SCALEPACK** suite (HKL-2000 / HKL-3000) with [[Wladek Minor]] - the dominant data-processing software used for X-ray diffraction at synchrotron MX beamlines worldwide through the 2000s-2010s.

## Key Relationships
- [[Wladek Minor]] -- lifelong HKL co-developer, [[University of Virginia]]
- HKL-2000 / HKL-3000 suite -- processed a majority of PDB depositions over two decades
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member (Read et al., Structure 2011)
