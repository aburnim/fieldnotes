> [!info] Info
> Max Planck Research Group Leader at the Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter (MPSD) in Hamburg. Develops methods to obtain higher-resolution electron densities from protein crystals by going beyond Bragg peaks, using continuous diffraction and diffuse scattering. Also works on algorithms for understanding conformational dynamics from XFEL data.
