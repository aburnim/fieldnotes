[[Global Phasing Ltd]]

> [!info] Info
> Founder and Director of [[Global Phasing Ltd]] in Cambridge, UK (established 1996). Senior figure in crystallographic phasing and refinement methodology. Principal architect of **SHARP** (Statistical Heavy Atom Refinement and Phasing) and the **BUSTER-TNT** maximum-likelihood refinement package. Long-standing advocate for rigorous statistical treatment of crystallographic data.
