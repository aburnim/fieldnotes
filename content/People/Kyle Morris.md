#leader #cryo-em #pi #wwpdb #archive-lead

[[EMDB]] / [[EMBL-EBI]]

> [!info] Info
> Team Leader for the Electron Microscopy Data Bank ([[EMDB]]) at [[EMBL-EBI]]. Brings ten years of first-hand cryo-EM experience from research and from imaging and structural biology service facilities. His vision for EMDB focuses on strengthening core archival functions, building connections to complementary databases, and preparing the archive for future cryo-EM and cryo-ET technologies (including volumeEM).

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Principal Investigator for [[EMDB]], the electron microscopy archive within the [[wwPDB]] consortium.

## Affiliations
- [[EMBL-EBI]] -- Team Leader, Electron Microscopy Data Bank ([[EMDB]])

## Career
- Returned to the UK in 2018; staff scientist at the MRC Laboratory of Medical Sciences (LMS), London - established and embedded cryo-EM and volumeEM imaging at the institute
- 2021: moved to the UK national electron microscopy facility at [[Diamond Light Source]] as eBIC (electron Bio-Imaging Centre) Training Coordinator, responsible for user training programmes
- Now: EMDB Team Leader, EMBL-EBI

## Committees & service
- [[wwPDB]] -- Principal Investigator (EMDB)

## Key Relationships
- [[EMDB]] has been operated jointly by [[EMBL-EBI]] and [[RCSB PDB]] since 2007, and became a full [[wwPDB]] member on January 1, 2021. Kyle Morris leads the team that succeeded founding director Kim Henrick.
- eBIC background connects him to the [[Diamond Light Source]] cryo-EM community and the MRC LMS imaging community
