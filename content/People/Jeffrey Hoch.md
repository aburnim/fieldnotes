[[BMRB]] / [[University of Connecticut]]

> [!info] Info
> Professor in the Department of Molecular Biology and Biophysics at [[University of Connecticut|UConn Health]] (Farmington, CT). Serves as Director of the Biological Magnetic Resonance Data Bank ([[BMRB]]), the NMR archive within the [[wwPDB]] consortium; Director of the Gregory P. Mullen NMR Structural Biology Facility; Director of the National Center for Biomolecular NMR Data Processing and Analysis (NMRbox.org); and Director of the Network for Advanced NMR (NAN). NMRbox is a freely available virtualisation platform providing hundreds of NMR software packages preconfigured in a single environment - a response to fragmentation in biomolecular NMR tooling.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Principal Investigator for [[BMRB]], the NMR member body of the [[wwPDB]]. BMRB was co-founded at the [[University of Wisconsin Madison]] in 1988 by [[John Markley]] (founding PI) and [[Eldon Ulrich]] (founding operational director). Hoch took over and oversaw the archive's relocation to UConn Health, Farmington.
