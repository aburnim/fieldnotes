[[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] / [[ALS]]

> [!info] Info
> Senior scientist at the Molecular Biophysics and Integrated Bioimaging (MBIB) Division, [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]]. Long-standing contributor to [[PHENIX]] and the Computational Crystallography Toolbox (cctbx), and a core developer of [[DIALS]] - particularly for XFEL (serial femtosecond crystallography) data processing at LBNL.

> [!faq] Handoff context
> Transitioned from PHENIX core development into XFEL / serial crystallography data processing through DIALS. Co-recipient of the 2021 Berkeley Lab Tech Transfer Award for the Phenix team.
