[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] / [[MRC-LMB]]

> [!info] Info
> Group Leader in the Structural Studies Division at the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology|MRC-LMB]], Cambridge. Develops and maintains [[RELION]], the open-source cryo-EM image processing package used by laboratories worldwide for atomic-resolution structure determination. Current research focuses on the structural biology of neurodegeneration: how amyloid filaments form in the brain and relate to disease. The group has resolved over 20 distinct tau conformations across different tauopathies, demonstrating that a single protein can adopt many amyloid structures.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Advisory Committee member, [[EMDB]] representative. Brings the RELION user community and the cryo-EM validation perspective to wwPDB governance.
