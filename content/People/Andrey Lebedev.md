[[CCP4]] / [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]]

> [!info] Info
> Scientific Programmer at [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory|STFC Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]], [[CCP4]] funded developer. Author of **Zanuda**, the CCP4 program that addresses space-group and origin ambiguity in macromolecular structures with pseudo-symmetry and twinning. Co-developer (with [[Eugene B. Krissinel]]) of the CCP4 Cloud platform and CCP4 Package Manager.
