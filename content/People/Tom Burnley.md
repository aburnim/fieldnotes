#cryo-em #computational #software-dev

CCP-EM / [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]]

> [!info] Info
> Staff member at CCP-EM (Collaborative Computational Project for Electron cryo-Microscopy), based at [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory|STFC Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]], Harwell. CCP-EM is the cryo-EM sibling of [[CCP4]], co-founded by [[Martyn Winn]] in 2012. Burnley works on software and methods support for the UK cryo-EM community.
