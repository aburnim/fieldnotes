#mx #computational #software-dev #glycoscience

Justus-Liebig-University Giessen

> [!info] Info
> Institute of Veterinary Physiology and Biochemistry, Justus-Liebig-University Giessen, Germany. Developer of **pdb-care** (PDB Carbohydrate REsidue check), a validation tool for complex carbohydrate/glycan structures in PDB files. Roughly 30% of PDB entries containing carbohydrates historically had errors; pdb-care was a foundational tool for glycan validation, with the lineage continuing through [[PDB-REDO]] glycan handling and Privateer.

## Key Relationships
- **pdb-care** / CARP -- carbohydrate validation tools predating [[PDB-REDO]]'s glycan support
- Glycan-validation community connection to [[Anastassis Perrakis]] and [[Robbie P. Joosten]] on [[PDB-REDO]]
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member (Read et al., Structure 2011)
