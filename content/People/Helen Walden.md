> [!info] Info
> Professor of Structural Biology at the Institute of Molecular Cell and Systems Biology, University of Glasgow. Research focuses on molecular mechanisms of protein ubiquitination - particularly the E3 ligase FANCL (mutated in Fanconi Anemia) and Parkin (mutated in early-onset Parkinson's). Member of the [[CCP4]] Executive Committee.
