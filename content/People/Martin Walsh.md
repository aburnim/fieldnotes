[[Diamond Light Source]] / [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]]

> [!info] Info
> Deputy Director of Life Sciences at [[Diamond Light Source]], deputy to Life Sciences Director David Stuart. Coordinates Diamond's life science activities spanning macromolecular crystallography, small-angle X-ray scattering, circular dichroism, infrared microspectroscopy and soft X-ray imaging for biology. Also a Research Group Leader at the Research Complex at Harwell (RCaH). Over 30 years experience in macromolecular crystallography with more than 1,000 structures deposited in the PDB. Joined Diamond in January 2009 from the MRC France (Institut Laue-Langevin / EMBL Grenoble area).
