[[Osaka University]] / [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)]]

> [!info] Info
> Professor at the Institute for Protein Research (IPR), Osaka University, where he leads the Laboratory of Protein Crystallography / Division of Protein Structural Biology. Became Head of [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]] in April 2017, succeeding [[Haruki Nakamura]]. Appointed the 22nd Director of the Institute for Protein Research on April 1, 2024. Research interests include photosynthetic electron transport proteins and structural biology of protein assemblies.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Principal Investigator for [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]], the Japanese member of the [[wwPDB]] consortium. Leads PDB operations for Asia, and currently supports PDBc (China) through shared infrastructure.
