[[Netherlands Cancer Institute]] (NKI)

> [!info] Info
> Group Leader at the Netherlands Cancer Institute (NKI) in Amsterdam, where he leads an integrative structural biology group focused on the cellular mechanisms of cancer. His team integrates X-ray crystallography, small-angle scattering, cryo-EM, NMR, and biochemical/biophysical methods in vitro and in cells. Best known for co-developing the ARP/wARP software package for automated model building in X-ray crystallography (cited more than 5,000 times) and for leading the PDB-REDO initiative, which re-refines and improves the models in the public PDB archive.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Advisory Committee member, [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]] representative. (Listed in 2025 AC regrets but is an active member.) PDB-REDO feeds model improvements back into wwPDB validation conversations.

## Training
- DPhil Chemistry, [[University of York]] (1996); pre-doctoral research at [[EMBL Hamburg]]
- EMBO long-term fellow, NKI (DNA mismatch recognition and repair)
- Staff scientist and team leader, EMBL Grenoble (developed ARP/wARP)
- Returned to NKI as independent group leader around 2000

## Software
- **ARP/wARP** -- automated model building in X-ray crystallography (1999, with team at NKI); >5,000 citations
- **PDB-REDO** -- server and databank that re-refines and rebuilds PDB structures for improved quality

## Key Relationships
- PDB-REDO is closely connected to the wwPDB validation pipeline and used by deposition pipelines across [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)]]
- [[Victor Lamzin]] ([[EMBL Hamburg]]) -- long-running ARP/wARP collaborator
