#mx #computational

University of Southampton / [[CCP4]]

> [!info] Info
> Professor of Structural Biology at the University of Southampton. Uses crystallography to study molecular interactions with a particular interest in large dynamic macromolecular complexes and time-resolved techniques. Known for work on PLP synthase (vitamin B6 biosynthesis) and on dual-active enzymes including the c-di-GMP enzyme MorA. Serves as **2023-28 Chair of CCP4 Working Group 1** (the MX software working group).

## Key Relationships
- [[CCP4]] Executive Committee -- 2023-28 Chair of Working Group 1
- University of Southampton Structural Biology group
- PI on "New horizons in computational structural biology with CCP4" grant
