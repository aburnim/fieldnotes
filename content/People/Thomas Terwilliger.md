> [!info] Info
> Laboratory Fellow at [[Los Alamos National Laboratory]] and Senior Scientist at the [[New Mexico Consortium]]. One of the founding developers of [[PHENIX]], co-leading the multi-institutional consortium with [[Paul Adams]] ([[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]]), [[Randy Read]] ([[University of Cambridge|Cambridge]]), and [[Jane S. Richardson]] ([[Duke University|Duke]]). Principal author of SOLVE/RESOLVE for experimental phasing and density modification, now embedded in PHENIX.
