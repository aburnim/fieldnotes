#mx #computational #software-dev

[[CCP4]] / [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]] / University of Liverpool

> [!info] Info
> CCP4 funded developer at [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory|STFC Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]] with a collaborative affiliation to the Institute of Integrative Biology, University of Liverpool. Primary developer of **MrBUMP**, the automated molecular replacement pipeline that takes a target sequence and reflection data, prepares search models, and passes them to [[Phaser]] or MOLREP. MrBUMP originated in the e-HTPX project and has been updated to leverage AlphaFold-predicted models.
