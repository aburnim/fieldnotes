#computational #pharma

Genentech

> [!info] Info
> Computational chemist and long-career drug discovery leader at Genentech (Discovery Chemistry), South San Francisco. A prominent figure in structure-based and ligand-based drug design, chemoinformatics, and pharma-academia interactions in computational chemistry. Per the 2016 Ligand Validation Workshop paper he represented Genentech at the 2015 workshop.

## Key Relationships
- Genentech Discovery Chemistry
- Long-running member of the industrial computational chemistry community overlapping with [[Anthony Nicholls]] (OpenEye) and [[Jeffrey Bell]] (Schrödinger)
- [[wwPDB task force-Ligand Validation Workshop|Ligand Validation Workshop]] 2015 attendee
