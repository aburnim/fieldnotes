Cambridge Crystallographic Data Centre (CCDC)

> [!info] Info
> Executive Director of the Cambridge Crystallographic Data Centre ([[CCDC]]) at the time of the 2015 Ligand Validation Workshop. CCDC is the UK organization that maintains the Cambridge Structural Database (CSD) - the global archive of small-molecule crystal structures and the reference source for bond-length/angle restraints used across macromolecular crystallography. CCDC develops Mercury (visualization), GOLD (docking), and CSD-based ligand-geometry libraries.
