#mx #computational #software-dev

[[University of Cambridge]] / CIMR / [[PHENIX|PHENIX Consortium]]

> [!info] Info
> Principal Research Associate at the Cambridge Institute for Medical Research (CIMR), [[University of Cambridge]]. Lead day-to-day developer of **Phaser**, the maximum-likelihood molecular replacement program distributed via [[PHENIX]] and [[CCP4]]. At CIMR since 2000, making her the longest-tenured active Phaser developer. First author of the 2007 "Phaser Crystallographic Software" paper that established the modern Phaser codebase.

> [!faq] Handoff context
> McCoy is the current-generation Phaser lead under [[Randy Read]]'s group. She represents the practical continuity of the Phaser project from its origins through the modern cryo-EM and AlphaFold era.
