> [!info] Info
> Principal Investigator at [[Diamond Light Source]], leading the XChem fragment screening platform. Professor of Structural Chemical Biology at the [[University of Oxford]] and Principal Investigator at the [[Structural Genomics Consortium]] (SGC) Oxford. Pioneer of high-throughput crystallographic fragment screening for drug discovery and open science in early-stage drug discovery.
