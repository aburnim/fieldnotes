> Scientist in the MBIB Division at [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]] and core [[PHENIX]] developer. Contributor to PHENIX real-space refinement and to the riding hydrogen model for joint X-ray and neutron refinement. Central to hydrogen handling and restraint infrastructure in PHENIX.
