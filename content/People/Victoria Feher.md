UC San Diego / Drug Design Data Resource (D3R)

> [!info] Info
> Project Scientist at the Drug Design Data Resource (D3R) at UC San Diego. D3R runs the Grand Challenges, blind community benchmarks that evaluate structure-based drug design methods against newly-released protein-ligand co-crystal structures. D3R is a natural partner to [[wwPDB]] on ligand-structure quality.

## Key Relationships
- D3R Grand Challenges -- ligand-pose prediction benchmarking platform
- UC San Diego -- host institution
- [[wwPDB task force-Ligand Validation Workshop|Ligand Validation Workshop]] 2015 co-organizer
- Links academic/industry drug discovery community to [[wwPDB]] validation discussions
