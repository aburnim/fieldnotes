[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]]

> [!info] Info
> Retired scientist from the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology|MRC-LMB]], Cambridge. Developer of **MOSFLM**, the integration program for macromolecular crystallography data that was the standard at MRC-LMB and many UK synchrotron beamlines before [[DIALS]]. [[CCP4]] developer/contributor (per ccp4.ac.uk/about/people/).
