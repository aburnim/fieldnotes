SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory / Stanford

> [!info] Info
> Staff scientist at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory. Computational biophysicist working at the intersection of molecular dynamics simulation, X-ray scattering, and XFEL science. Co-author of **MDTraj**, the widely-used open-source Python library for analysis of molecular dynamics trajectories (with Pande group). Research connects MD simulations to experimental scattering observables, including work on extracting structural dynamics from X-ray solution scattering and time-resolved XFEL experiments.

> [!faq] DiffUSE relevance
> Lane's expertise in connecting MD simulations to X-ray scattering data is directly relevant to DiffUSE's core scientific question: understanding how protein conformational dynamics manifest in diffuse scattering patterns. His SLAC position gives access to XFEL and synchrotron infrastructure for time-resolved experiments.
