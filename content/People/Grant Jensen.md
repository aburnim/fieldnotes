> [!info] Info
> Professor at Brigham Young University (BYU), formerly at Caltech. Pioneer of cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET) for in situ structural biology. Known for cryo-ET studies of bacterial and archaeal ultrastructure, and for creating open educational resources for cryo-EM/cryo-ET.
