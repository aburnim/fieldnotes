#mx #computational #software-dev

University of Liverpool

> [!info] Info
> Professor at the Institute of Integrative Biology, University of Liverpool. Lead of **AMPLE** (Ab initio Modelling of Proteins for moLEcular replacement), a joint Liverpool / [[CCP4]] software development that uses clustering and truncation of ab initio models to generate molecular replacement search models. Member of the [[CCP4]] Executive Committee.
