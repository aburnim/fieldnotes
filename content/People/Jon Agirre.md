#mx #cryo-em #computational #software-dev #glycoscience

[[University of York]] / York Structural Biology Laboratory

> [!info] Info
> Royal Society Olga Kennard Research Fellow at the York Structural Biology Laboratory (YSBL), Department of Chemistry, [[University of York|University of York]]. Lead developer of **Privateer**, the carbohydrate structure validation, refinement, and analysis tool - described as "the Swiss Army knife for carbohydrate structure validation." Privateer has been routinely used in SARS-CoV-2 structure determination that informed mRNA vaccines and inhibitor design. Serves as **CCP4 Working Group 2 co-chair**.

## Key Relationships
- [[CCP4]] Executive Committee -- WG2 co-chair
- **Privateer** -- lead developer; successor in spirit to [[Thomas Lütteke]]'s pdb-care for glycan validation
- Glycan validation community: [[Thomas Lütteke]] (pdb-care historical), [[PDB-REDO]] glycan handling
- Co-author on the 2023 CCP4 suite publication
