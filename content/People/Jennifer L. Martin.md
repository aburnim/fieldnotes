#leader #mx #wwpdb

[[University of Queensland]] / Griffith University

> [!info] Info
> Professor Emerita at the [[University of Queensland]] (Institute for Molecular Bioscience) and Adjunct Professor at Griffith University. Established the first protein crystallography laboratory in Queensland in 1993, which evolved into the UQ Remote Operation Crystallisation and X-ray Diffraction (UQ ROCX) Facility, of which she was the Foundation Director. From 2019 to 2022 she served as Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Research and Innovation) at the University of Wollongong. Research expertise: structural biology, protein crystallography, protein interactions, and applications to drug design and discovery, particularly against drug-resistant bacteria. Leading advocate for gender equity and diversity in science.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> IUCr Representative on the [[wwPDB]] Scientific Advisory Committee. Brings the international crystallographic community perspective and structured advocacy for data-sharing standards.
