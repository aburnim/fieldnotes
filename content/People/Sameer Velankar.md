[[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)]] / [[EMBL-EBI]]

> [!info] Info
> Section Head for Macromolecules, Structure, Chemistry and Bioimaging at [[EMBL-EBI]]; Senior Scientist at [[EMBL]]; leader of the Protein Data Bank in Europe ([[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]]) team. Career focus: enabling use of macromolecular structure data through advanced data analysis, query, and visualisation tools. Leads work on the PDB, the PDBe-KB (Knowledge Base), and the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (developed collaboratively with [[Google DeepMind]]).

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Principal Investigator for [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]], the European member of the [[wwPDB]]. Key architect of wwPDB validation, visualisation, and the AlphaFold DB integration.
