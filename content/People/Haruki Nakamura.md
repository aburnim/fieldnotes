[[Osaka University]] / [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)]]

> [!info] Info
> Professor Emeritus at [[Osaka University]] Institute for Protein Research and founding Head of the Protein Data Bank Japan ([[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]]). Led PDBj from its establishment until handing over to [[Genji Kurisu]] in April 2017. Central figure in the transnational launch of [[wwPDB]] in 2003 (alongside [[Helen M. Berman]] at RCSB and the EBI founding team).

- [[wwPDB task force-Ligand Validation Workshop|Ligand Validation Workshop]] 2015 co-organizer
