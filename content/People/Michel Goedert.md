#neurodegeneration

[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] / [[MRC-LMB]]

> [!info] Info
> Joint Head of the Neurobiology Division at the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology|MRC-LMB]], Cambridge. Pioneer of research on tau protein and its role in Alzheimer's disease and other tauopathies, and on alpha-synuclein in Parkinson's disease. Long-standing collaborator with [[Sjors Scheres]] on cryo-EM structures of amyloid filaments from patient brain tissue, which revealed that different tauopathies are defined by distinct filament conformations.
