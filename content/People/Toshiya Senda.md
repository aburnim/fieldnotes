#leader #mx #beamline #wwpdb

[[Osaka University]] / Photon Factory, KEK

> [!info] Info
> Professor and Director of the Structural Biology Research Center (SBRC) at the Photon Factory, Institute of Materials Structure Science, High Energy Accelerator Research Organization (KEK) in Tsukuba, Japan. Joined KEK in January 2013 and became director of SBRC. SBRC has approximately 40 members split between beamline operation/development and structural biology research (including cryo-EM), and operates five MX beamlines at the Photon Factory: BL-1A, BL-5A, BL-17A, AR-NW12A, and AR-NE3A. SBRC pioneered a long-wavelength MX beamline (BL-1A) for native single-wavelength anomalous diffraction (native-SAD) phasing.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Advisory Committee member, [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]] representative. Brings expertise in beamline automation, native-SAD phasing, and Japanese synchrotron infrastructure to wwPDB governance.
