#mx #computational #software-dev

CMBI, Radboud University Nijmegen

> [!info] Info
> Retired Professor of Computational Molecular Biology / Bioinformatics at the Centre for Molecular and Biomolecular Informatics (CMBI), Radboud University Nijmegen Medical Centre. Developer of **WHAT_CHECK** and **WHAT IF** - foundational protein structure validation tools that predated MolProbity and PDB-REDO. Also associated with **YASARA** (molecular modeling and dynamics).

## Key Relationships
- **WHAT_CHECK** / **WHAT IF** -- early protein structure validation; scores still used in wwPDB validation reports
- [[Robbie P. Joosten]] -- Joosten trained at CMBI with Vriend before moving to [[Anastassis Perrakis]] and [[PDB-REDO]]
- [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]] member (Read et al., Structure 2011)
