#leader #cryo-em

Chan Zuckerberg Imaging Institute

> [!info] Info
> Director of Cryo-EM at the Chan Zuckerberg Imaging Institute (CZII), Redwood City, CA. Previously co-director of the National Resource for Automated Molecular Microscopy (NRAMM) at the [[New York Structural Biology Center]] and Scripps. Pioneer of automated cryo-EM data acquisition (Leginon, Appion), which enabled the high-throughput single-particle cryo-EM revolution.
