#cryo-em #cryo-et

[[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology]] / formerly [[EMBL]]

> [!info] Info
> Joint Head of the Structural Studies Division at the [[MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology|MRC-LMB]], Cambridge (moved from [[EMBL]] Heidelberg in 2017). Leading developer and user of cryo-electron tomography (cryo-ET) and subtomogram averaging, applied to membrane trafficking, viral assembly, and cellular ultrastructure.

## Training lineage
- At EMBL Heidelberg, trained many of today's cryo-ET leaders including [[Giulia Zanetti]] (Birkbeck, wwPDB AC member)
