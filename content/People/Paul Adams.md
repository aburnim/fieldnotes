[[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] / [[ALS]]

> [!info] Info
> Associate Laboratory Director for Biosciences at [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory|LBNL]], overseeing the Biological Systems & Engineering, Environmental Genomics & Systems Biology, and Molecular Biophysics & Integrated Bioimaging (MBIB) Divisions, as well as the DOE Joint Genome Institute. Senior Scientist and Director of the MBIB Division since 2015. Vice President for Technology and Director of Structural Biology for the Joint BioEnergy Institute (JBEI) since 2007. Leads the PHENIX software consortium for automated solution of macromolecular structures using crystallographic and cryo-EM methods. Adjunct Professor of Bioengineering at [[UCSF|UC Berkeley]] (a common appointment for LBNL scientists) since 2007.

> [!faq] wwPDB role
> Advisory Committee member, [[RCSB PDB]] representative. Bridges the US archive side of the [[wwPDB]] with the PHENIX software community and the Berkeley structural biology infrastructure.

## Training
- PhD in Biochemistry / Structural Biology, University of Edinburgh (interest in protein simulation and parallel computing)
- Postdoctoral fellow, Yale University (central developer of the CNS - Crystallography and NMR System - software)

## Career milestones
- Joined [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] in 1999
