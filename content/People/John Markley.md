[[University of Wisconsin Madison]] / [[BMRB]]

> [!info] Info
> Steenbock Professor of Biomolecular Structure at the [[University of Wisconsin Madison]] and founding director of the Biological Magnetic Resonance Data Bank ([[BMRB]]) when it was established in 1988. BMRB moved to [[University of Connecticut|UConn Health]] under [[Jeffrey Hoch]] after Markley's directorship. Central figure in solution NMR methodology and the NMR structure archive's governance integration into the [[wwPDB]].

## Key Relationships
- [[BMRB]] -- founding director
- [[Jeffrey Hoch]] -- successor as BMRB director
- [[Eldon Ulrich]] -- long-time BMRB operational leader at Madison
- [[wwPDB task force-Ligand Validation Workshop|Ligand Validation Workshop]] 2015 co-organizer
