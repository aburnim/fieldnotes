#nmr #computational

NIH / NIDDK

> [!info] Info
> Chief, Laboratory of Chemical Physics at the NIH National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK). Pioneer of solution NMR methods for protein structure determination, especially for large systems via paramagnetic relaxation enhancement (PRE) and residual dipolar couplings (RDCs). Co-developer of the Xplor-NIH NMR structure calculation package with [[Charles Schwieters]].
