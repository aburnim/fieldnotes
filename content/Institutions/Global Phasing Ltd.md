> [!info] Info
> Global Phasing Ltd is a private crystallography software company based in Cambridge, UK. Founded by [[Gerard Bricogne]] (former MRC-LMB). ~8 person team. Develops BUSTER (refinement), autoPROC (data processing), SHARP (phasing), and GRADE (ligand restraints). Funded by a consortium of ~15 pharmaceutical/biotech companies. Free for academic use (registration required).

> [!warning] Commercial consortium model
> Unlike [[CCP4]] (publicly funded) or [[PHENIX]] (NIH funded), Global Phasing operates on a private consortium model where pharma companies fund development in exchange for early access. This is a distinct governance pattern in the landscape.

## Key Relationships
- Competes with [[PHENIX]] and [[CCP4]] for refinement (BUSTER vs. phenix.refine vs. REFMAC5)
- autoPROC competes with XDS, DIALS for data processing
- [[Ian Tickle]] (Global Phasing) also appears on [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|X-Ray Validation Task Force]]
- STARANISO online service widely used for anisotropic data analysis
