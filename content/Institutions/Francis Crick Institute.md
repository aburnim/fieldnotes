> [!info] Info
> The Francis Crick Institute is a biomedical research centre in London, UK. Opened 2016 as a partnership between the MRC, Cancer Research UK, Wellcome, UCL, Imperial, and King's. Houses major cryo-EM and structural biology facilities.
