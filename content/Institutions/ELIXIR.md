> [!info] Info
> ELIXIR is an inter-governmental organisation that coordinates life science data resources across Europe. Hub-and-spoke model: central Hub at Wellcome Genome Campus (Hinxton, UK, alongside [[EMBL-EBI]]) with 23 national bioinformatics Nodes. Established as an EMBL Special Project with ring-fenced budget. Fourth five-year scientific programme commenced 2024. [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]] is an ELIXIR Core Data Resource.

## Funding
- EMBL member states (core)
- EU Horizon 2020 / Horizon Europe (EXCELERATE, CONVERGE, EOSC-Life, GDI projects)
- National funding through member state Nodes
- UKRI (BBSRC, MRC, NERC) for ELIXIR-UK

## Governance
- ELIXIR Board (governing body, member state representatives)
- ELIXIR Director (reports to Board)
- Legally an EMBL Special Project with separate accounts/auditing
- 5 Platforms: Data, Tools, Compute, Interoperability, Training

## Key Relationships
- [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]] is an ELIXIR Core Data Resource
- Signed strategic collaboration with [[Instruct-ERIC]] (Nov 2024) on FAIR data, AI-ready repositories
- Co-located with [[EMBL-EBI]] at Wellcome Genome Campus
- 13 shared member states with [[Instruct-ERIC]]
- ELIXIR 3D-BioInfo Community connects structural biology data resources
