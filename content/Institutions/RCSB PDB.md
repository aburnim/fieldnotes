# Collaborations 
#### [[wwPDB]]

The Worldwide Protein Data Bank ([wwPDB](http://wwpdb.org/)) was formed to maintain a single PDB archive of macromolecular structural data that is freely and publicly available to the global community. It consists of organizations that act as deposition, data processing and distribution centers for PDB data. As the US Data Center, RCSB PDB biocurates structures submitted from the Americas.  

#### [[PDB-IHM]]

[PDB-IHM](https://pdb-ihm.org/) is a system for archiving structural models obtained using integrative or hybrid modeling, managed by the wwPDB and funded by the NSF, DOE, and NIH.

#### [[EMDataResource]]

[EMDataResource](https://www.emdataresource.org/) provides access to 3DEM density maps and metadata, news, events, software tools, data standards, and validation methods.  

#### [[KBase]]

[KBase](https://www.kbase.us/) enables users to analyze, share, and collaborate using data and tools designed to help build increasingly realistic models for biological function. KBase utilizes RCSB PDB APIs to provide users with access to PDB data.

#### [[Nucleic Acid Knowledgebase]]

[NAKB](https://nakb.org/) contains information about experimentally-determined nucleic acids and complex assemblies.
