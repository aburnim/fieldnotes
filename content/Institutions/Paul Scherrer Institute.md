> [!info] Info
> The Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI) is Switzerland's largest research institute for natural and engineering sciences, located in Villigen. Operates the Swiss Light Source (SLS) and SwissFEL. The SLS MX beamlines are major European facilities for macromolecular crystallography.
