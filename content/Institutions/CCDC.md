> [!info] Info
> The Cambridge Crystallographic Data Centre (CCDC) maintains the Cambridge Structural Database (CSD), the global archive of small-molecule crystal structures. CSD-derived bond-length and angle restraint libraries are used across macromolecular crystallography in [[PHENIX]], [[CCP4]], and [[wwPDB]] validation pipelines. CCDC also develops Mercury (visualisation), GOLD (docking), and ligand-geometry tools.
