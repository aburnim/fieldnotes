> [!info] Info
> The National Institute of Immunology (NII) in New Delhi is an autonomous research institute funded by the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India. Focus areas include immunology, infection, reproduction, and structural/computational biology. Hosts the NII Bioinformatics Centre and the RiPPMiner resource.
