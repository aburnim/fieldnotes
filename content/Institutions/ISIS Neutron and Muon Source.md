> [!info] Info
> ISIS is a pulsed neutron and muon source at the [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]], Harwell, UK. Operated by STFC. Provides neutron scattering instruments for studying materials, including single-crystal diffuse scattering capabilities. Co-located with [[CCP4]] and [[Diamond Light Source]] on the Harwell campus.
