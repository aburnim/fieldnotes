> The Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) is a US nonprofit medical research organisation that funds investigators embedded at universities and research institutions across the country. HHMI Investigators in the structural biology landscape include [[Axel T. Brunger]] ([[Stanford University]]), [[Yifan Cheng]] ([[UCSF]]), [[David Baker]] ([[University of Washington]]), and many others.
