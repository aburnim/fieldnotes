> [!info] Info
> CCP-EM is the cryo-EM equivalent of [[CCP4]], supporting software development and distribution for the cryo-EM community. Based in the UK, funded by [[MRC]] and [[BBSRC]]. Provides a python-based pipeliner that integrates [[RELION]] and other cryo-EM tools.

## Key Relationships
- Python pipeliner mimics [[RELION]] pipeliner, callable from RELION GUI
- Inter-compatibility with [[CCP4]] is a stated priority (CCP4 WG1 2024 minutes)
- Bridges the gap between cryo-EM processing ([[RELION]], [[cryoSPARC]]) and model building ([[Coot]], [[PHENIX]])

## Funding
