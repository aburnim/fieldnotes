> The University of Michigan in Ann Arbor hosts the Life Sciences Institute (LSI) and the Department of Biological Chemistry. Operates LS-CAT (Life Sciences Collaborative Access Team), a major macromolecular crystallography beamline sector at the [[APS|Advanced Photon Source]].
