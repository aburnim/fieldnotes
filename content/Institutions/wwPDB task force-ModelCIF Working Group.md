Active Development 
[ModelCIF](https://github.com/ihmwg/ModelCIF/blob/master/dist/mmcif_ma.dic) is an extension of the [PDBx/mmCIF](http://mmcif.wwpdb.org/) dictionary. The [PDBx/mmCIF](http://mmcif.wwpdb.org/) dictionary is used by the [wwPDB](http://www.wwpdb.org/) to archive structural models of macromolecules determined using X-ray crystallography, NMR spectroscopy and cryogenic electron microscopy.

https://github.com/ihmwg/ModelCIF
