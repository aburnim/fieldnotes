> [!info] Info
> Instruct-ERIC is a pan-European distributed research infrastructure for integrated structural biology. Designated an ERIC (European Research Infrastructure Consortium) in July 2017. 17 member countries plus EMBL. Hub in Oxford, UK. Provides funded access to 25+ structural biology facilities across Europe. Part of ESFRI roadmap since 2006.

## Funding
- Member state national contributions
- EU Horizon Europe grants (iNEXT-Discovery, ISIDORe, canSERV)
- Access funded for researchers in member countries

## Key Relationships
- Strategic collaboration with [[ELIXIR]] (signed Nov 2024)
- 13 shared member states with ELIXIR; EMBL as shared partner
- Includes [[CEITEC]] (Czech Republic) as an Instruct Centre -- also key for [[Mol*]] development
- Provides access to cryo-EM facilities including [[Diamond Light Source]] eBIC
- Connects to [[wwPDB]] through depositions from Instruct-funded research
