> [!info] Info
> EMDB is the global public archive of 3D electron microscopy maps of biological specimens. Founded in 2002 at [[EMBL-EBI]] (Hinxton, UK). As of 2025, contains >50,000 entries. Became a [[wwPDB]] Core Archive in 2021, making the EMDB team a core wwPDB member and the wwPDB-designated Archive Keeper for EMDB.

> [!faq] Key Relationships
> - Core Archive of [[wwPDB]] (since 2021)
> - Located at [[EMBL-EBI]], same campus as [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]]
> - Companion archive: [[EMPIAR]] (raw EM image data), also at EMBL-EBI with mirror at [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]]
> - Depositions managed through [[wwPDB]] OneDep system
> - Biocuration split geographically: [[RCSB PDB]] (Americas/Oceania), PDBe/EMDB team (Europe/Africa), PDBj/PDBc (Asia)
> - [[RCSB PDB]] also acts as deposition/distribution center under NIH Unified Data Resource for CryoEM
> - Software ecosystem: data processed with [[RELION]], [[cryoSPARC]], [[CCP-EM]] and others; visualized with [[ChimeraX]], [[Mol*]]
