> [!info] Info
> Radboud University Nijmegen, Netherlands, hosts the Centre for Molecular and Biomolecular Informatics (CMBI) at the Radboud University Medical Centre. CMBI has been an important node in European structural bioinformatics, historically associated with the development of [[WHAT_CHECK]] / WHAT IF protein validation tools.
