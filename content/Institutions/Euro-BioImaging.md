> [!info] Info
> Euro-BioImaging is a European research infrastructure for biological and biomedical imaging. ERIC status since 2019. Provides open access to imaging technologies including cryo-EM, light microscopy, and medical imaging across European facilities. Complements [[Instruct-ERIC]] (structural biology focus) with a broader imaging scope.

## Key Relationships
- Overlaps with [[Instruct-ERIC]] for cryo-EM access
- Complements [[BioImage Archive]] at [[EMBL-EBI]] for data archiving
- Part of the ESFRI Biological and Medical Sciences cluster
