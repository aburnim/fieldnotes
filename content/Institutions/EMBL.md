> [!info] Info
> EMBL is an intergovernmental research organization with 28 member states. Headquartered in Heidelberg, Germany. Six sites across Europe: Heidelberg, Barcelona, Grenoble, Hamburg, Hinxton (EMBL-EBI), and Rome. Parent organization of [[EMBL-EBI]], which in turn houses [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]], [[EMDB]], [[EMPIAR]], [[AlphaFold DB]], [[BioImage Archive]], and is home to the [[ELIXIR]] Hub.

> [!faq] Why EMBL matters for the landscape
> EMBL is the institutional umbrella under which many of the key European structural biology data resources operate. Understanding EMBL's governance helps explain the co-location and tight integration of PDBe, EMDB, EMPIAR, AlphaFold DB, ELIXIR, and 3D-Beacons at the Hinxton campus, and SASBDB/ATSAS at Hamburg.
