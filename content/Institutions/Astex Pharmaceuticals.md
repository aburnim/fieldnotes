> [!info] Info
> Cambridge UK and Pleasanton USA-based biopharmaceutical company (formerly Astex Therapeutics, now Astex Pharmaceuticals, subsidiary of Otsuka). Pioneer of fragment-based drug discovery using high-throughput X-ray crystallography. The Astex fragment-screening platform was foundational for modern fragment-based methods now used at [[Diamond Light Source]]'s XChem and by the [[Structural Genomics Consortium]].
