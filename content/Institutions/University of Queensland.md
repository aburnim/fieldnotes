> The University of Queensland is a public research university in Brisbane, Australia. Its Institute for Molecular Bioscience (IMB) hosts a long-standing protein crystallography community and the UQ Remote Operation Crystallisation and X-ray Diffraction (UQ ROCX) Facility.
