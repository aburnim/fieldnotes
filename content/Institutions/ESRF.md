> [!info] Info
> The European Synchrotron Radiation Facility (ESRF) in Grenoble, France is one of the world's brightest X-ray sources. Operates MX, SAXS, and inelastic scattering beamlines used across structural biology and materials science. Completed the EBS (Extremely Brilliant Source) upgrade in 2020.
