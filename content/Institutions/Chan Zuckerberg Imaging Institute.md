> The Chan Zuckerberg Imaging Institute (CZII) in Redwood City, CA is a division of the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative focused on developing and applying advanced imaging technologies for biology. Hosts significant cryo-EM infrastructure and develops open-source software tools.
