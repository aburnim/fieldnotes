> [!info] Info
> The University of Oxford is a collegiate research university in Oxford, England. Hosts a strong structural biology community across the Department of Biochemistry, the Nuffield Department of Medicine, and allied institutes on the Old Road Campus in Headington. Close ties to [[Diamond Light Source]] and the [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]] at the Harwell Science and Innovation Campus.

## Key Relationships
- Host institution for the [[Structural Genomics Consortium]] (SGC) Oxford site
- [[Frank von Delft]] -- Professor of Structural Chemical Biology, joint with [[Diamond Light Source]] and SGC
- Strong collaborative links with [[Diamond Light Source]] (XChem, MX, eBIC)
