> [!info] Info
> CCPN was established in 1999 in the UK to create a common data standard for NMR, develop open-source NMR software, and organize community meetings. Coordinated by [[Ernest Laue]] at the University of Cambridge. Software suite includes AnalysisAssign, AnalysisScreen, AnalysisMetabolomics. Governed by an executive committee elected by UK NMR groups.

## Key Relationships
- Sibling CCP to [[CCP4]] and [[CCP-EM]]
- Close collaboration with [[BMRB]] (Jeffrey Hoch is a CCPN partner)
- Partners include [[Alexandre Bonvin]] (Utrecht), [[Gaetano Montelione]] (RPI)
- CCPN data model complements NMR-STAR format used by [[BMRB]]
- Advocates for mandatory NMR data deposition to [[BMRB]]
