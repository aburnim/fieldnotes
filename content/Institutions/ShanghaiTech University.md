> [!info] Info
> ShanghaiTech University in Shanghai, China, hosts the School of Life Science and Technology (SLST), the iHuman Institute, and the Shanghai Institute for Advanced Immunochemical Studies. Closely associated with the National Facility for Protein Science in Shanghai, which is the organizational home of [[PDBc (China, ShanghaiTech)|Protein Data Bank China]].

## Key Relationships
- [[PDBc (China, ShanghaiTech)|PDBc]] -- China's [[wwPDB]] associate-member archive is co-located here
- [[Wenqing Xu]] -- Tenured Full Professor SLST; wwPDB AC Associate Member representative for China
- iHuman Institute -- structural biology of GPCRs and other membrane receptors
