> [!info] Info
> The Max Planck Institute for the Structure and Dynamics of Matter (MPSD) in Hamburg, Germany investigates the structure and dynamics of matter using ultrafast spectroscopy, XFEL, and condensed matter theory. Located on the DESY campus alongside [[EMBL Hamburg]].
