> [!info] Info
> Scipion is an open-source framework for integrating multiple cryo-EM image processing packages into unified workflows. Developed at the [[CNB-CSIC]] (Centro Nacional de Biotecnologia, Madrid). Integrates [[RELION]], [[cryoSPARC]], [[EMAN2]], [[CTFFIND]], and others.
