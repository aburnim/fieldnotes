> [!info] Info
> The BioImage Archive at [[EMBL-EBI]] stores and distributes biological images useful to life science researchers. Provides data archiving services to the broader bioimaging community including [[EMPIAR]], Cell-IDR, and Tissue-IDR. Built on EMBL-EBI's scalable object store infrastructure (UKRI SPF funded).

## Key Relationships
- [[EMPIAR]] runs on BioImage Archive infrastructure
- Complements [[EMDB]] (3D maps) and PDB (atomic models) in the data ecosystem
- Integration with [[Euro-BioImaging]] for cross-modality data
