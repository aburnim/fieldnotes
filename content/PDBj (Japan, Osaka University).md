PDBj (Protein Data Bank Japan) maintains the single global PDB/BMRB/EMDB archives of macromolecular structures and provide integrated tools, under the Joint Usage and Research activities of the Institute for Protein Research. The database is supported by [JST-NBDC](https://biosciencedbc.jp/en/ "NBDC") (JPMJND2205) and [AMED-BINDS](https://www.binds.jp/ "AMED-BINDS"). 



### Funding 
PDBj supported by JST NBDC and is a member of Japan alliance for Bioscience Information (JBI Portal)
