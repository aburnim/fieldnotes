## Structural Biology Landscape

### Data Archives and Governance

The [[wwPDB]] is the central governing body for structural biology data. Its
member archives are the definitive repositories for the field:

- [[RCSB PDB]] — US data center (Rutgers/UCSD/UCSF), largest single funding
  stream (~$38M, NSF/DOE/NIH)
- [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)]] — European data center, also leads [[AlphaFold DB]]
  and [[3D-Beacons]]
- [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)]] — Asia-Pacific data center
- [[PDBc (China, ShanghaiTech)]] — Newest wwPDB member
- [[BMRB]] — NMR spectroscopy data archive (UConn Health)
- [[EMDB]] — Electron microscopy map archive (EMBL-EBI)
- [[EMPIAR]] — Raw cryo-EM image archive

The [[PDB-IHM]] extension handles integrative and hybrid models, representing
the frontier of multi-method structural data.

### Key Institutional Power Centers

- **EMBL-EBI (Hinxton, UK)** — Houses [[PDBe (UK, EMBL-EBI)|PDBe]], [[EMDB]],
  [[EMPIAR]], [[AlphaFold DB]], [[BioImage Archive]], and the [[ELIXIR]] hub.
  Highest institutional concentration in the field.
- **Lawrence Berkeley National Lab (USA)** — Home to [[PHENIX]], [[DIALS]],
  and [[ALS]] beamlines. Bridges data collection, processing, and refinement.
- **MRC-LMB (Cambridge, UK)** — Origin of [[RELION]], [[Coot]], and REFMAC5.
  Core open-source cryo-EM and crystallography tools.
- **UConn Health (USA)** — Runs [[BMRB]], [[NMRbox]], and NAN. Single
  institution controlling most NMR data infrastructure.
- **UCSF (USA)** — [[ChimeraX]] development, RCSB PDB site, integrative
  modeling via [[Dr. Andrej Sali]].

### Facilities and Light Sources

Synchrotrons and beam lines are where experimental data is collected:

- [[APS]] — Advanced Photon Source (Argonne, USA)
- [[CHESS]] — Cornell High Energy Synchrotron Source (recently awarded $20M NSF)
- [[SSRL]] — Stanford Synchrotron Radiation Lightsource
- [[ALS]] — Advanced Light Source (Berkeley, USA)
- [[Diamond Light Source]] — UK national synchrotron (Wellcome + STFC)
- [[NSLS]] — National Synchrotron Light Source (Brookhaven, USA)

### Software Ecosystem

#### Crystallography
- [[PHENIX]] — Dominant refinement suite (NIH + commercial royalties)
- [[CCP4]] — UK collaborative computational project (BBSRC funded)
- [[DIALS]] — Modern open-source data processing
- [[Coot]] — Model building (single developer, MRC-LMB)
- [[XDS]] — Widely used, single developer, zero dedicated funding

#### Cryo-EM
- [[RELION]] — Open-source, community standard (GPLv2)
- [[cryoSPARC]] — Commercial competitor to RELION
- [[EMAN2]] — Alternative processing suite
- [[cisTEM]] — Lightweight alternative
- [[CTFFIND]] — CTF estimation (used across all pipelines)
- [[Scipion]] — Workflow integration framework
- [[SerialEM]] — Microscope control (single developer, controls most cryo-EM
  scopes worldwide)

#### NMR
- [[CCPN]] — Collaborative computing project for NMR
- [[SPARKY]] — Spectral analysis
- [[NMRPipe]] — Processing pipeline (NIST, single developer)
- [[ARIA and CYANA]] — Structure calculation

#### Visualization
- [[ChimeraX]] — UCSF, free for academic use
- [[PyMOL]] — Schrödinger, commercial
- [[Mol*]] — Web-based, open-source (EMBL-EBI + RCSB PDB)

#### Validation
- [[MolProbity]] — Structure validation (Duke, single NIH grant)
- [[ModelAngelo]] — Automated model building for cryo-EM

### Standards and Governance Bodies

- [[IUCr]] — International Union of Crystallography (founded 1947)
- [[NIAC]] — NeXus data format governance at synchrotrons
- [[ELIXIR]] — European life science data coordination (23 member states)
- [[Instruct-ERIC]] — Pan-European structural biology infrastructure
- [[History of mmcif working group]] — Origins of modern data standards
