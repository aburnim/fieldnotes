[[UCSF]] / [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]]

> [!info] Info
> Ernest L. Prien Professor and Chair, Department of Bioengineering and Therapeutic Sciences, [[UCSF]]. Faculty Scientist, Molecular Biophysics and Integrated Bioimaging Division, [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]]. Associate Director, Quantitative Biosciences Institute (QBI). PhD with Tom Alber at UC Berkeley (protein dynamics and crystallography). Started independent career at UCSF in 2011. Full Professor since 2020; Department Chair since August 2023.
>
> Pioneer in extracting conformational ensemble information from X-ray diffraction data. Lab spans structural biology, deep mutational scanning, and drug discovery. Strong commitment to open science (former ASAPbio board member). Co-host of "Tortured Proteins" podcast with [[Stephanie Wankowicz]].

> [!faq] DiffUSE role
> Jaime Fraser is a DiffUSE PI lead. His lab collects data at [[ALS]] Beamline 8.3.1 (directed by [[James Holton]]). The Fraser Lab is the origin of qFit (multi-conformer modeling software that forms the backend of Sampleworks) and has deep expertise in protein conformational dynamics -- the core scientific question DiffUSE aims to address.
