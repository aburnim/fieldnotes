> [!info] Info
> CCP4 was established in 1979 in the UK to support collaboration between researchers developing software for macromolecular X-ray crystallography. Core team is based at the Research Complex at Harwell ([[RCaH]]) at [[Rutherford Appleton Laboratory]] (Didcot, near Oxford). Funded by the UK [[BBSRC]] (Biotechnology and Biological Sciences Research Council). The suite includes ~250 programs and is distributed to academic and commercial users worldwide.

> [!faq] Governance Structure
> - **Working Group 1 (WG1)**: meets annually at the Study Weekend; sets general policy. Open to UK structural biology PIs, experimental officers, and commercial license holders.
> - **Working Group 2 (WG2)**: forum for active crystallographic developers; oversees meetings and organizes the annual Study Weekend.
> - **Executive Committee**: Chairs of WG1 and WG2, CCP4 Group Leader, and elected WG1 members. Day-to-day oversight.

## Funding
- Primary: [[BBSRC]] grants (currently 4-year cycle)
- Hosted by STFC Computational Science and Engineering Dept
- Commercial license revenue from industrial users
- CCP4 is free for academic use

> [!warning] Funding dynamics
> CCP4 submitted a new 4-year BBSRC grant (discussed at WG1 2024). Inflation has pressured planning. Growing emphasis on AI/ML and interoperability with [[CCP-EM]].


## Key Relationships
- Tight coupling with [[CCP-EM]] (cryo-EM equivalent)
- Software overlap with [[PHENIX]] (both do refinement, model building)
- Many CCP4 contributors also participate in [[wwPDB task force-X-Ray Validation|wwPDB X-Ray Validation Task Force]]
- [[Paul Adams]] sits on both CCP4 and PHENIX advisory structures
- CCP4 programs used within [[wwPDB]] OneDep pipeline
