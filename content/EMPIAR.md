> [!info] Info
> EMPIAR is the public archive for raw 2D electron microscopy images underpinning 3D cryo-EM maps in [[EMDB]]. Also accommodates volume EM and X-ray tomography datasets. Launched 2012 (proof-of-principle) at [[EMBL-EBI]]. As of 2026, contains ~2,800 entries totaling ~8.2 PiB. All data CC0 license (fully open). Mirror site at [[PDBj (Japan, Osaka University)|PDBj]].
