> [!info] Info
> The Advanced Light Source (ALS) is a synchrotron facility at [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] (Berkeley, CA). DOE Office of Science user facility. Currently undergoing ALS-U upgrade for improved brightness. Hosts multiple structural biology beamlines. Home institution of [[Paul Adams]] ([[PHENIX]]) and [[Aaron Brewster]] ([[DIALS]], [[NIAC]] Chair).

> [!faq] LBNL as a landscape nexus
> [[Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory]] houses ALS (beamlines), [[PHENIX]] ([[Paul Adams]]), [[DIALS]] ([[Aaron Brewster]]), and the RCSB PDB UCSD collaboration. [[Paul Adams]] is also Assoc. Lab Director for Biosciences at LBNL. This concentration of data processing, refinement, and data format expertise makes LBNL a key node.
